What we are building

The problem statement names two pain points, not one — Ayurvedic innovation is under-protected, and Indian traditional knowledge is exposed to misappropriation abroad. Our two agents map onto those two clauses exactly. Most teams will read only the first.

How the two agents are orchestrated

One agentic RAG shell, two agents inside it

Not two products bolted together. An Orchestrator reads intent, then a Depth Controller decides how hard to work — so a one-second question never costs twelve seconds of agent loop.

QUICK

Classifier + one retrieval pass

The default. “Is this patentable?”

GUIDED

+ graph traversal + profile engine

“Show me the full IP map.”

DEEP

+ external tools, multi-hop search

“Find similar filings globally.” On demand only.

Depth is decided automatically, never asked upfront. We answer at QUICK and offer escalation under the answer. Asking how deep to go adds a step to every query including the trivial ones, and most people cannot say what depth they want before seeing an answer.

Verification is its own node, not a line in the prompt

Most teams write “cite your sources” into the prompt, which means the model grades its own homework. We pull it out as a separate deterministic step — NLI entailment of each generated sentence against the span it cites. That is what makes the citation guarantee real rather than aspirational.

Prahari is the exception to all of this: a second entry point, fired by a scheduler, never by a query.

Agent 1 · Sahayak सहायक

The IP Protection Map

The unit of interaction is a Formulation Dossier, not a question. The agent returns the protectable surface across all seven IP regimes, each marked open or barred with the citation that decides it.

An MSME believes “I can’t patent it, so I have nothing.” The truth is they have five other doors. That reframe is the product.

The line the demo ends on

“Patent is barred by Section 3(p). But you have five open doors, and here is the order to file them in.”

Agent 2 · Prahari प्रहरी

The unprompted sentinel

Registering a formulation silently registers its species to a watchlist. The user never supplies a patent number — if they had to, this would be a ChatGPT prompt with extra steps.

We watch at publication, not at grant

An application is published under Section 11A at 18 months. From that moment until grant, Section 25(1) lets any person file a pre-grant opposition — Form 7A, Rule 55. And no patent may be granted before six months from publication, so every alert carries a computable deadline.

Before grant, stopping a bad patent costs a form. After grant it costs a lawsuit. Turmeric took India years and crores precisely because nobody was watching at publication.

  • Acts unprompted. No query initiates it.
  • Holds state and diffs against it. “New since last run” is the system’s decision, not the user’s.
  • Decides what deserves attention. Most hits are noise; a scorer picks the few worth a human’s time.
  • Produces an artefact, not a reply. A prior-art dossier assembled from evidence the user never supplied.

A national watchlist of ~50 top dravya runs with zero users, giving AIIA a standing dashboard. That is what makes it a ministry instrument rather than a user tool.

Non-negotiables

Never accuse anyone

A third-party observation does not accuse — it submits prior art that may be relevant to examination. That is the legally correct framing and it is also our liability shield.

User-facing output says “prior art potentially relevant to this application.” The words biopiracy, misappropriation, stolen and theft never appear in the UI. Human review is mandatory before anything leaves the system. No auto-filing, ever.

No target metrics on any slide

Measured values only. A measured 0.71 with an error analysis beats a claimed 0.90 without one — and it is the only version that survives Q&A.

Every citation verified before it reaches a slide

The product’s premise is that it never fabricates authority. The team holds itself to the same standard. Every section number is checked against India Code by Himanshi first.

Prahari + Evaluation

40% of the AI/ML work by volume, 100% of the resume-bearing artifacts: the calibration model, the evaluation methodology, and the claim-matching logic.

Prahari · the sentinel

W2

Patent Office Journal parser

Fetch and parse the weekly Journal PDF from ipindia.nic.in, published every Friday under Section 145. Filter on IPC A61K36/* — the Journal carries IPC, not CPC. Pull ~8 recent issues for the demo. Confirm the IPC field appears as expected before building on it.

W2

Species resolution

Expand claimed binomials through POWO/IPNI and GBIF into synonym sets, cached locally. Naive string matching misses obsolete names — this step is why the matching works.

W2

Claim-vs-prior-art entailment

mDeBERTa-v3-xnli scores each independent claim against candidate Formulary passages. Does the Formulary already teach this? Same model later powers citation-faithfulness scoring and Examiner View — one model, three jobs.

W2

Opposition-window computation

earliest_grant = published_on + 6 months, then days_remaining. Rank alerts by urgency × risk, not risk alone — a closing window outranks a marginally better match. Unit-test against hand-checked entries, including one already granted and one early-published via Form 9.

W2

Form 7A generator

A Section 25(1) representation under Rule 55, carrying Formulary citations in the form Sharangadhara Samhita, Madhyama Khanda 7/40–43. For human review — never auto-filed.

W4

Scheduler, push, national watchlist

Friday cron sweep, diff against seen application numbers, Supabase realtime notification. National watchlist of ~50 dravya so it works with zero users.

W4

Foreign stream stretch

Only if week 2 finished early. Google Patents BigQuery, CPC A61K36/*, non-Indian applicants → third-party observations under EPC Art. 115 / US 37 CFR 1.290 / PCT via ePCT.

Evaluation · built by agents from your spec

This lane is the most precisely specifiable component in the project, which makes it the best candidate for Claude Code agents to build. Write the spec; review the diffs. It should not consume your hours directly — that is how we remove you as a single point of failure on the differentiators.

W1

Minimal harness

50 questions, faithfulness + accuracy. Enough to steer week 1 development.

W4

Span-anchored gold set

Generate backwards from the source. Start from a statute chunk, generate the question it answers. The ground-truth span is then guaranteed by construction — which yields a retrieval gold set for free, so nDCG@10 and recall@20 become computable without anyone labelling relevance. Hand-written questions give an answer set only.

W4

Judge calibration

Cohen’s κ against ≥50 human-labelled answers before the LLM judge is trusted for any reported number. An uncalibrated judge is a vibe with a number attached.

W4

Calibrated abstention gate

Logistic / GBM over retrieval features — max cosine, reranker score, NLI entailment, source tier — predicting citation-faithfulness. Powers the problem statement’s required confidence indicator with a model instead of a heuristic. Yields a reliability curve and ECE.

Reported metrics · the PS's own four axes

  • Answer accuracy — gold set + BhashaBench-Ayur, LLM judge with κ reported
  • Citation correctness — per-sentence NLI entailment against the cited span; separately, does the cited section exist and say what was claimed
  • Safe abstention — precision on the out-of-scope subset, plus ECE
  • Multilingual quality — per-language delta vs. English

Not fine-tuning a reranker

It costs 5–6 days, may lose to off-the-shelf bge-reranker-v2-m3 — turning the headline into a negative result — and is invisible to judges. Measuring retrieval properly is the stronger signal at one-fifth the cost.

Sahayak + Knowledge Graph

60% of the AI/ML work. You own the advisory agent end to end — corpus through to the IP Protection Map — so there are almost no handoffs in the critical path.

The query path

W1

Orchestrator + Depth Controller

Extract intent and entities, then triage to QUICK / GUIDED / DEEP. Auto-triage — never ask the user upfront. Escalation affordances render under the answer instead. A QUICK query that triggers DEEP is a cost bug, not just a latency bug, so this gets its own routing-accuracy test.

W1

Verification Agent

A separate deterministic node, not a line in the prompt. NLI entailment of each generated sentence against the span it cites, plus the guardrail rails. Its output decides Confidence OK vs abstain to human. Note: NLI, not NLU — different thing, and a technical judge will notice.

Retrieval foundation

W1

Corpus ingestion

Pull ~15 acts from the India Code mirror via its JSON API. Chunk, embed with bge-m3, store in pgvector against Saima’s corpus_version table. Every chunk keeps its official indiacode.nic.in URL as the citation of record.

W1

Hybrid retrieval + rerank

BM25 + dense, then bge-reranker-v2-m3. Run the three-way bake-off so the numbers exist from day one.

W1

Grounded generation

Mandatory citations, enforced by the NeMo Guardrails output rail — uncited legal claims are blocked, not warned about. Every answer carries its corpus version stamp, which is what later lets us flag stale answers. Jurisdiction toggle keeps India and treaty answers visibly separate.

Knowledge graph

W2

AFI Part I extraction

Parse the archive.org OCR text into ≈120 formulations. Use the metadata and transliterated names — not the Devanagari, which OCR’d poorly. Each formulation keeps its source-text citation.

W2

Schema and edges

nodes(id, type, props jsonb) and edges(src, dst, type, props, confidence, provenance), traversed with recursive CTEs. Every edge carries provenance and confidence — nothing enters the graph without a citable source. Same rule the product enforces on its own answers.

W3

Graph query API

Feeds both the Cytoscape viz and the specificity that upgrades answers: “contains Withania somnifera → 3 ABS obligations → 2 GI conflicts → 41 global filings.”

The advisory product

W3

Formulation classifier

Six classes, logistic head over bge-m3 embeddings, labels bootstrapped from AFI plus rules. ~6 hrs. Load-bearing — it is the routing decision the whole system hangs on, and it produces a confusion matrix and macro-F1.

W3

Regulatory-profile engine

Rules as typed, cited, versioned YAML. The runtime match is a pure function — deterministic, unit-testable, no LLM variance, no hallucinated requirement. The LLM only explains the gap in plain language.

Same schema serves national obligations and international market access. One engine, two jobs — less code, not more. Adding a market is a data file, not a code change.

W3

IP Protection Map

All seven regimes with open/barred verdicts and the deciding citation. Patent usually barred under Sec 3(p), or Sec 3(e) for polyherbals — mere admixture is the provision that actually kills these applications, and nobody discusses it.

W3

Examiner View + cultivator view

Examiner View fires automatically, and only when the patent verdict is “may be open” — a risky verdict owes the user an explanation of why an examiner might disagree, and because it runs on the minority case it costs nothing on the common path. Grounded in the IP Office’s own draft AYUSH guidelines, which require synergy greater than the sum of the components to survive Sec 3(e). Cultivator view answers “I grow X in district Y” → GI eligibility, ABS posture, PPV&FR route.

Research

Three deliverables, each a checklist rather than an open-ended search. Verification, not authoring — which is a far easier task and the one that actually gets finished.

Deliverable 1 · source verification · day 1

One row per source. For each: does the URL resolve, what format is behind it, what licence applies, and how many items are there. Do not summarise the content — just confirm access.

D1

Highest priority — blocking

The Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India. cdn.ayush.gov.in did not resolve on 10 Sept. Try pcimh.gov.in, then Internet Archive. Report which volumes you can actually download. If none, we proceed on the Formulary alone — but we need to know on day 1, not week 3.

D1

Are the AYUSH patent guidelines final?

The IP Office issued Draft Guidelines for processing patent applications of AYUSH systems on 5 Feb 2025, comments closed 28 Feb 2025. Find out whether a final version has since been published. Until you confirm, we must label them “draft guidance” everywhere — presenting draft guidance as settled law would break the product’s own premise.

D1

Patent Office Journal — is it downloadable?

Published every Friday on ipindia.nic.in. Download two recent issues. Confirm: does each entry carry an IPC code, an applicant name and a publication date, and is the layout the same between the two weeks? Dia’s parser depends on the answer.

D1

Bhashini registration

Register on the ULCA portal and generate an API key. Registration latency is a tracked risk — start it before anything else.

D1

Confirm the statute list

For each of ~15 acts, confirm it exists in the India Code mirror and note its identifier. Patents Act 1970 and the 2024 Rules; Biological Diversity Act 2002 as amended 2023 and the 2024 Rules; GI, Trade Marks, Designs, Copyright, PPV&FR; Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 and Rules 1945; DMR (Objectionable Advertisements) Act 1954; FSS Act 2006.

Deliverable 2 · regulatory profile fields

Fill a fixed template — the fields are given, you find the values and the citation for each. US and EU only. Every field needs the specific article or rule it came from.

  • Registration route · evidence years required · dossier components
  • Clinical trials required (yes/no) · permitted claims · prohibited claims
  • Competent authority · form identifier and URL · required attachments
  • Citation for every single field. A field without a citation is left blank, not guessed.

Also ~30 ABS rows from NBA materials, and ~20 Ayurveda-relevant entries from the GI Registry.

Deliverable 3 · gold-set verification

You are not writing 200 questions. The system generates them from statute text; you verify a stratified sample of about 60 and mark each correct or incorrect.

Why a sample is enough

The sample’s error rate becomes the reported quality bound on the whole set. That is statistically legitimate, it is what a careful team would actually do, and it is one-third of the work of checking all 200.

For each item: does the cited section exist, and does it actually say what the answer claims? Those are two separate checks and both matter — a real section cited for something it does not say is the failure mode we most need to catch.

The standing rule

Nothing unverified reaches a slide

The product’s entire premise is that it never fabricates authority. Every section number, rule number and treaty article the team uses is checked against India Code by you first. If we get one wrong in front of AIIA judges — who know this law — the citation-grounding claim collapses on the spot.

Every statutory reference on this page is a pointer for verification against India Code, not an authority. Dia holds the full specification; each lane above is complete on its own.
